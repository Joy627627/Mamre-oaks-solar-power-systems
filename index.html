PM SURYA GHAR

Government subsidy guidance

For eligible residential households, the current PM Surya Ghar CFA structure provides ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000/kW for additional capacity up to 3 kW, with the total subsidy capped at ₹78,000 for systems above 3 kW.

Eligibility, process and vendor requirements apply. We will help you understand the applicable process; subsidy approval is subject to the official scheme and DISCOM requirements.
